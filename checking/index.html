Deadlines. Notebook, Assignment and Book are checked before the next class of the same subject in that class (Hindi-1 and Hindi-2 count as one Hindi class). The test notebook is due before the 2nd test of any subject after it, because students alternate two test notebooks, so test notebooks are priority. A lesson spread over several days is received on its last day. Dates come from the Academic Planner (Classes 6–8): 15–20 Oct from the October planner, 21–31 Oct from the revised planner, including Maths (Class 6 homework notebook and assignments, and Maths tests; Classes 7–8 written work is the CM’s content notebook). Teachers tap their own box in a subject tab, enter Defaulters and Absentees (when the task asks for them), and save. Logins: each teacher sets their own password once and a manager approves it once. Reports are accepted only from that teacher (or a manager) for their own sections, carry their name and the server time, and lock at the end of the day. Every change is logged in the Google Sheet. Only managers (Editors) can add, edit or remove tasks. Editing: “+ Add task” adds any notebook, test, assignment, checking material, extra task, preparation, class or school task for chosen sections. “Edit” on any job changes its name, dates, sections, teacher and whether a Defaulter/Absentee report applies, or removes it. Removed planner jobs can be restored from the bottom of the subject tab.
